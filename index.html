About me

I'm a Computer Science and Engineering student at SRM University in Andhra Pradesh, India. I was born in Khulna, Bangladesh, and moved here to study. I live in the university hostel. My main focus is cybersecurity and ethical hacking, and I work as an independent bug bounty researcher across YesWeHack, HackerOne, Bugcrowd and HackenProof.

In the security community I go by the code name dr.hackersmm006, and that is the name on my GitHub.

Outside security, I compete in programming contests, draw, write stories and poems, and have taught and organised events. My long-term plan is an M.Tech in cybersecurity at an international institution.

How I work

I don't drop a problem until it's solved. That habit is why puzzles, CTFs and math appeal to me, and why my bug reports include the full path from finding to fix.

I'm always learning something new, and I take exams and projects seriously enough to plan them well ahead.
